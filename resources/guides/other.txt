Register the key on {{HOST}} as {{USERNAME}}:
  1. Sign in to {{HOST}} as {{USERNAME}} and open the SSH keys page of your account settings.
  2. Add a key: name it after this machine, for example "gitum {{PLATFORM_DIR}}", and paste the public key.
  3. Save it.
gitum test only checks that {{HOST}} accepts the key: it does not know this platform's greeting.
Then run: gitum test {{PLATFORM_DIR}} {{USERNAME}}
