Register the key on GitLab as {{USERNAME}}:
  1. Open {{SETTINGS_URL}} and sign in as {{USERNAME}}.
     (Avatar > Edit profile > SSH keys)
  2. Click "Add new key".
  3. Key: paste the public key.
  4. Title: a name for this machine, for example "gitum {{PLATFORM_DIR}}".
  5. Usage type: "Authentication". Expiration date: optional.
  6. Click "Add key".
Then run: gitum test {{PLATFORM_DIR}} {{USERNAME}}
