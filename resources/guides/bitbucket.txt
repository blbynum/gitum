Register the key on Bitbucket as {{USERNAME}}:
  1. Open {{SETTINGS_URL}} and sign in as {{USERNAME}}.
     (Settings cog > Personal Bitbucket settings > Security > SSH keys)
  2. Click "Add key".
  3. Label: a name for this machine, for example "gitum {{PLATFORM_DIR}}".
  4. Key: paste the public key.
  5. Expiry: "No expiry", or a date. The default is 365 days.
  6. Click "Add key".
Then run: gitum test {{PLATFORM_DIR}} {{USERNAME}}
